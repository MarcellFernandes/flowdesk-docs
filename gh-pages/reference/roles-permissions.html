Roles and Permissions Reference

Access Control Overview

FlowDesk enforces role-based permissions to maintain operational integrity, accountability, and security. Each role is granted capabilities aligned with its service responsibilities.

Permissions Matrix

Capability User Technician Manager
Create tickets Yes Yes Yes

Contribute to knowledge base No Yes Yes
Manage knowledge base No Limited Full control

Governance Considerations

Permissions are enforced automatically to prevent unauthorized actions and preserve auditability. Organizations may refine role policies according to internal governance requirements.
